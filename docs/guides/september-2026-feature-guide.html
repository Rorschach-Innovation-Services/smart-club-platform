Part Two

What clubs see

For club chairpersons and representatives. Three things look different.

Fixtures now read like a conventional fixture list

Open Fixtures. Each row now shows the full matchup home-first — Saints Cricket Club A vs Spartan Sporting CC — instead of splitting your side, the opponent and a home/away marker across separate columns. Both clubs are named in full, your own side highlighted in bold, and every row lines up on the vs so the list reads cleanly top to bottom. The Home / Away pill stays alongside. A club fielding several sides sees each named in full (A, B, C) exactly where it plays; a match between two of a club's own sides appears once, home side first — Saints Cricket Club A vs Saints Cricket Club B.

If a club asks why it changed

The old table listed your side, the opponent and a home/away flag in three places. A fixture list everyone already reads the same way — home team, then the away team — needs none of that decoding.

Veterans squad: find a player, ask their club

A Veterans squad item appears in the left-hand menu, but only for clubs the union has fixtured into a veterans league. Nobody is added to a squad without their own club agreeing.

Find

Type at least three letters of a name into Find a player. The club sees the player's name and their club, nothing else. Players already attached to a veterans club, already registered in a veterans league, or the searching club's own players, do not appear.

Request

Click Request, optionally with a short note. The row shows Requested and the request appears under Pending requests, where it can be withdrawn. The other club's chairperson receives an email.

The other club decides

On their Players page a card reads Crusaders CC asks to register Thabo Ndlovu for veterans cricket, with Accept and Decline. The player's row on the roster shows a Veterans request badge until they act.

Done

On accept the player appears in the veterans club's Veterans affiliates list and under Recent outcomes. The player stays registered at their own club. Neither club's player count changes.

What accepting does not do

It does not move the player, change either club's numbers, or start a transfer. It records that this person plays veterans cricket for that club, the same thing a player records when they declare it at registration.

Leagues the union entered for you

If the union office enters a club into a league outside its district (an EMCU junior league for an Ilembe club, for example), step three of the affiliation form shows it under Entered by the union. The club cannot untick it, and re-saving the form or changing district will not drop it. Only the union office can remove it.

Part Three

What the union office sees

For union administrators. Four screens changed.

Entering a club into another district's league

Clubs → the club → Leagues. Below the district's own leagues is a collapsed Leagues from other districts. Expand it, tick the leagues, Save leagues. Cross-district leagues stay visible with their district name even when the section is collapsed.

Dawnheights

Clubs → Dawnheights Cricket Club → Leagues → expand → tick Under 11 and Under 13 under Ethekwini Metro Cricket Union → Save. One side each is the default.

Veterans Requests

A new Veterans Requests menu item with a count of pending requests. It lists every request in the union with filters for pending, accepted, declined and withdrawn. Clubs normally resolve these themselves; the office should Accept or Decline here only to unblock a stuck request. A decline asks for an optional reason, which the requesting club sees. Office decisions are marked as such and both chairpersons are emailed.

Insights and the Excel export

Per-league counts still count clubs that are entered in a league, not clubs that happen to have fixtures. A league with fixtures but no entrants now shows a small note asking the office to have the platform team correct it. Export Excel gains Teams by league (one row per side, with club, district and chair contact) and Clubs by league (one row per club per league, with the number of sides and their names).

Changing a chairperson

Clubs → the club → Edit chairperson. Enter the new name, email and cell. When the name differs from the current chair, a note explains that the previous person's ID number and term dates will be cleared; the club re-enters them on its affiliation form. Notices such as clearance alerts go to the new contact immediately.

This does not change who can log in

Portal access is separate. Go to Team & Access, invite the new chair as a representative of the club, and remove the previous person's access.

Part Five

Good to know

The questions clubs and the office are likely to ask, with the answers.

"Can another club see my players' details?"

No. The veterans finder shows a player's name and club, nothing more. ID numbers, contact details and dates of birth never leave the union office. Every search is recorded. A club can only search once the union has actually fixtured it into a veterans league; ticking the league on its own form is not enough.

"Does accepting a request move the player?"

No. It notes that the player plays veterans cricket for the other club. Player counts, demographics and clearances are unchanged. If the player later transfers, the note follows the normal transfer process.

"We never got the email."

Request, accept and decline emails go to the chairperson's email address on the club's record. A club with no chair email gets no email, but the request still shows in the portal. Check the chairperson details in the admin console. There are no WhatsApp messages for this feature.

"Insights still shows zero for veterans."

Insights and the export count clubs that are entered in a league. Until the platform team has run the one-off correction for that union, the two veterans leagues read zero and their divisions are empty in the export. Every other league already shows correctly.

"Why did the unattributed player figure go up?"

A player with no recorded league is assumed to play in their club's only league. When a club gains a second league through the correction, that assumption no longer applies, so the unattributed figure can rise. It is the honest number, not a fault.

"Have the December fixtures moved yet?"

Not yet. Only Premier Men 50-Over (both halves) and Veterans Promotion 30-Over play on 12 and 13 December. Veterans Promotion already plays on 16 January, so its later rounds would each move one week. Nothing changes until the union confirms, and clubs will need to be told directly when it does.
